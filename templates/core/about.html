About My Trust

My Trust helps buyers check Facebook-based online sellers before paying, by collecting community reports, administrator-verified findings and feedback in one place.

Why it exists

Many small businesses sell through Facebook pages. Most are honest, but buyers also meet advance-payment scams, parcels that never arrive, fake products, pages impersonating known brands, and harassment after a complaint. These experiences are usually shared only in private chats, so the next buyer cannot learn from them.

How it works

  1. Report. A registered user reports a page with a description and screenshot evidence.
  2. Review. An administrator examines the evidence and approves, rejects or asks for more.
  3. Evaluate. Each page shows a trust score built from verified reports and community feedback.

How to read the information

Verified by admin
An administrator reviewed the evidence and confirmed the report.
Unverified allegation
Submitted by a user and not yet confirmed. It never changes the trust score.
Community feedback
Opinions from registered users, shown separately from verified findings.

Privacy

Evidence screenshots are visible only to the person who reported and to administrators. People are shown by username only; email addresses are never displayed publicly.

Limitations

  • All information is submitted by users. My Trust does not connect to or collect data from Facebook.
  • Administrator verification is a review of the evidence provided, not a legal judgement.
  • A page without reports is not necessarily safe. Be careful with advance payments to any seller.
  • The trust score is a transparent rule-based indicator, not a prediction.

My Trust is an undergraduate Software Engineering thesis project: Identification of Scam Patterns in Facebook-Based Online Sales Pages.
